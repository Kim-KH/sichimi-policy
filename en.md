# Sichimi (시치미) Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for **Sichimi** (the "App").

- **Effective date**: November 12, 2026
- **Developer**: koreapapa4040@gmail.com

---

## Summary

- The App stores the content of KakaoTalk notifications **only on your phone**.
- The developer **does not collect or transmit any information**. The App has no internet permission, so it technically cannot send data out.
- You can delete stored content at any time, and uninstalling the App removes everything.

---

## 1. Information the App accesses

When you grant **Notification Access**, the App reads the following from notifications shown by KakaoTalk (`com.kakao.talk`):

- Sender name
- Chat room name
- Message text shown in the notification
- Notification time

The App does not access photos, files or emoticons themselves; only the notice text (for example "sent a photo") is stored.
**Notifications from apps other than KakaoTalk are ignored and are not stored or processed.**

Stored messages may include information about the people you chat with. Please keep your device secure.

---

## 2. Purpose

The content is used only to provide these features:

- Re-reading messages without opening the KakaoTalk chat room
- Searching stored messages
- Showing messages that the sender later deleted, as they were saved
- Showing the unread count on the app icon

It is not used for advertising, analytics, sale, or sharing with third parties.

---

## 3. Storage and transmission

- Everything is stored only in the App's private storage on your phone.
- The App does not request internet access and sends no data to any server or other app.
- The App is excluded from cloud backup.

---

## 4. Retention and deletion

- In Settings you can choose a retention period (30 days / 90 days / 1 year / keep). Older messages are deleted automatically.
- You can delete by chat room, by message, or everything, from within the App.
- Uninstalling the App deletes all stored content.
- Turning off Notification Access stops new messages from being saved.

Deleting in the App removes only the copy saved by this App; your original KakaoTalk conversations are not affected.

---

## 5. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Notification access | The **core permission** for reading and saving KakaoTalk notification content. You turn it on in system settings and can turn it off at any time. |
| Post notifications (Android 13+) | Shows a notification with the unread **count** for the app icon. It contains no message content and is hidden on the lock screen. It can be turned off in Settings. |
| Check KakaoTalk installed | Only checks whether KakaoTalk is installed. The App does not read your list of installed apps. |

---

## 6. Settings and diagnostics stored on the device

The App also stores, on the device only:

- Retention period and icon-count settings
- Diagnostics for the "Check" screen: the time of the last KakaoTalk notification and the number of notifications with hidden content (no message content included)

---

## 7. Third parties

The App contains no ad networks, analytics, sign-in or payment SDKs. It uses these open-source libraries, which do not collect personal data:

- Expo, React Native
- @react-native-async-storage/async-storage (settings)
- expo-clipboard (copies selected messages only when you tap "Copy")
- react-native-safe-area-context (layout)
